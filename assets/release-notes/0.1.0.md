## Welcome to Guidy

Guidy turns your voice into text, entirely on your device.

- **Press your shortcut and speak.** Guidy records, transcribes with a local model, and pastes the text where your cursor is.
- **Live preview.** Streaming models show the transcript in the overlay as you speak.
- **Your models, your choice.** Download models from **Models**, or drop your own GGUF files into the models folder.
- **Optional polishing.** Turn on post-processing in **Advanced → Experimental** to clean up text with an LLM of your choice.
